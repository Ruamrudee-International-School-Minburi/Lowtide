The Old Lantern

Down past where the sunlight gives up, it was waiting: the fish the harbor only talks about after dark. It glows like a lamp someone forgot to switch off.

The town gets its legend back. You get the story.

How to play

Cast your hook and steer it with the mouse, your finger, or A/D.

On the way down, dodge everything. The first fish you touch bites, and you start reeling up.

On the way up, grab as many fish as your rig holds. Jellyfish knock one off your hook.

At the surface your catch flies into the air. Tap fish before they splash down to double their value.

Sell, upgrade, go deeper. Somewhere near the bottom, something glows.
